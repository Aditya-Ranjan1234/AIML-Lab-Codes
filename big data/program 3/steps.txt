Steps to run Program 3 (Pig Scripts):

1. Upload your dataset to HDFS (replace dataset3.csv with your actual file):
   hdfs dfs -put dataset3.csv /

2. Run any of the Pig scripts:
   pig script1.pig
   pig script2.pig
   pig script3.pig
   pig script4.pig
   pig script5.pig

Or run Pig interactively:
   pig

Then copy and paste the commands from the scripts into the Pig shell.
